## What "Highest Cash Back" Really Means (And Why Rates Vary So Much)

Cash back isn't a gift from the retailer. It's a slice of an affiliate commission. When you click from a portal to a store and buy, the store pays the portal a percentage of your order as a finder's fee. The portal then decides how much of that fee to keep and how much to send back to you.

That single decision is where the gap between portals opens up. Some sites keep a meaningful chunk and hand you the rest. Others pass the entire commission back and make their money elsewhere — through sponsored listings, ads, and volume bonuses from merchants. TopCashback sits firmly in the second camp. The company states on its homepage that it passes 100% of merchant commission back to members, which is also why it's comfortable slapping the word "highest" on a guarantee.

Rates still vary day to day and store to store because the underlying commission rates themselves fluctuate. A retailer might offer 8% on Monday and 4% on Friday. A portal running a promotional boost might temporarily offer double rates. So "highest cash back" is less a fixed number and more a moving target — and the smart play is knowing which portal consistently lands at the top of that target for the stores you actually shop.
